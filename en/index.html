How a meeting works

Three steps, and only the first one needs an account.

  1. Start a meeting

    Sign in with your email. We send a six-digit code; there is no password to forget or leak. Glugg makes a new link with a fresh key.

  2. Send the link

    Paste it into a calendar invite, a chat or an email. Whoever has the link can join, so share it the way you would share a door key.

  3. Talk

    Video goes straight between you when your networks allow it. When they don't, our server passes it along, still encrypted.

Privacy

What our server sees, and what it never can.

Calls are encrypted with AES-256 on a key derived from the link. Here is the whole list.

The server sees

  • A meeting numberA one-way hash of the key. It can't be turned back into the key.
  • Your IP addressWhile you are connected, because that is how calls connect.
  • The host's email addressOnly for people who sign in to start meetings.
  • How many are in the meetingAnd when each person joins and leaves.

It never sees

  • The keyBrowsers keep the part after # to themselves.
  • Your video and soundEncrypted before they leave your device, even when relayed.
  • The names you typeSent only to the others in the meeting, encrypted.
  • A recordingWe don't record calls, and without the key a recording would be noise.

Questions

Things people ask before the first call.

Do my guests need an account?

No. Anyone with the link can join with just a name. Only the person who starts the meeting signs in. If a guest arrives first, they wait until the host is there.

What stops someone else from joining?

The key in the link is 128 random bits, so it can't be guessed. Anyone you forward the link to can join, though. When everyone is in, the host can lock the meeting.

Can Glugg hand my calls to anyone?

We can only hand over what we have, and that is the list above. We never have the key, so we can't decrypt a call, live or later.

Why does my call say "relayed"?

Some networks, such as many mobile carriers and office firewalls, block direct connections. Then the video goes through our server in Norway. It is encrypted the same way.
