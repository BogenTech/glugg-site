How a meeting works

Three steps, and only the first one needs an account.

  1. Start or schedule

    Sign in with your email. We send a six-digit code; there is no password to forget or leak. Start a meeting now, or put it in the calendar.

  2. Invite

    Glugg emails invitations with a calendar file, and people answer with one click. You can also paste the link into a chat, or let people book a time themselves on your booking page.

  3. Talk

    Video goes straight between you when your networks allow it. When they don't, our server passes it along, still encrypted.

Privacy

What our server sees, and what it doesn't.

Calls are encrypted with AES-256 on your devices. Here are the main points. All of it is in the privacy policy.

The server sees

  • A meeting numberSo the right people end up in the same meeting.
  • Your IP addressWhile you are connected, because that is how calls connect.
  • The host's email addressOnly for people who sign in to start meetings.
  • How many are in the meetingAnd when each person joins and leaves.
  • Your meeting links, encryptedSo they are on all your devices. With Privacy mode, only your own devices can open them.

It doesn't see

  • Your video and soundEncrypted before they leave your device, even when relayed.
  • The names you typeSent only to the others in the meeting, encrypted.
  • A recordingWe don't record calls.

Questions

Things people ask before the first call.

Do my guests need an account?

No. Anyone with the link can join with just a name. Only the person who starts the meeting signs in. If a guest arrives first, they wait until the host is there.

What stops someone else from joining?

Every meeting link is unique and impossible to guess. Anyone you forward it to can join, though, so share it only with the people you want there. When everyone is in, the host can lock the meeting.

Can Glugg hand my calls to anyone?

We can only hand over what we have. We don't record calls, and video and sound are encrypted on your devices, so there is no call to hand over. What we keep is in the privacy policy.

What is Privacy mode?

The links to your meetings are kept encrypted with us, so they are on every device you sign in on. Turn on Privacy mode in Settings and only your own devices can open them. You then link a new device with a one-time code from one of the others.

Is there an app?

Yes, for iPhone and iPad. It is on TestFlight for now. Everywhere else you use the browser, with nothing to install.

Why does my call say "relayed"?

Some networks, such as many mobile carriers and office firewalls, block direct connections. Then the video goes through our server in the EU. It is encrypted the same way.
